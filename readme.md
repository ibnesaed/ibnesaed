## قاری و اسلامی محقق — Qari & Islamic Researcher

I recite and research the Quran — tilawat with tajweed discipline, paired with tafsir, hadith context, and the history behind the verse. This GitHub is where the developer side of that work lives: open-source tools, research notes, and datasets for Quranic study.

Everything here is cited. Sources: [Quran.com](https://quran.com), [Sunnah.com](https://sunnah.com), and named classical tafsir works — never unverified claims.

<br>

## 🕌 What's here

- **Tools** — tajweed reference utilities, verse cross-reference and transliteration scripts
- **Research notes** — structured tafsir comparisons and hadith cross-references, published openly
- **Datasets** — verse/ayah reference data for developers building Islamic-study tools
